# Business Insights

## Forecast Planning

The final forecast covers eight weeks from `2012-11-02` to `2012-12-21` across 45 stores.

The forecasted network sales total approximately $453.85M across the eight-week horizon.

## Seasonal Demand

Forecast demand increases around the Thanksgiving and year-end periods.

The forecast peak occurs during the week of `2012-12-21`, with approximately $72.86M in network sales.

Thanksgiving week, `2012-11-23`, has approximately $65.87M in forecast sales.

These periods require additional attention to inventory, staffing, and operational capacity.

## Store-Level Planning

Forecast demand is concentrated among the highest-volume stores.

Store 4 has the highest projected eight-week sales at approximately $20.35M.

High-volume stores should receive differentiated planning attention because errors at these locations have a larger effect on network-level performance.

## Forecast Accuracy

The final holdout WAPE is 4.64%.

Store-level error diagnostics can be used to identify locations where additional forecasting attention may be required.

## Decision Support

The forecasting system can support:

- Inventory allocation
- Staffing preparation
- Seasonal capacity planning
- Store-level demand prioritization
- Identification of locations requiring additional forecast review
